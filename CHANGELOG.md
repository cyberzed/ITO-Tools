# Changelog

What changed on the site, newest first. The site's **What's new** page is built from this file.

Versions follow `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`:

- **MINOR** for a new tool, page or feature. **PATCH** for a fix or a small wording change.
- **MAJOR** stays at 0 while the site is young.

Each version has a date and one or more of these sections: Added, Changed, Fixed, Removed.
Write each line for the people who use the site, not for developers.

## 0.5.4 - 2026-11-27

### Removed
- The Learning mode of the Marshals' table is taken out for now, so it can come back after a review. The Marshals' table works as in Exam mode.

## 0.5.3 - 2026-11-27

### Added
- Chief Marshal, Marshals' table: a Learning mode that checks each answer at once, with the reason and the rule. Exam mode is still the default and checks your answers when the athlete raises the bar.

## 0.5.2 - 2026-11-27

### Changed
- The front page is now about what the site is for and how it works. It no longer lists the tools and their pages, because the top bar and its drop down lists do that.

## 0.5.1 - 2026-11-27

### Added
- Each tool in the top bar has an arrow with a list of its pages, so you can go straight to a page. The list also opens when the mouse rests on the tool.

### Changed
- The tool in the top bar is no longer highlighted while you are on one of its pages. The page you are on is marked in the tool's own menu and in the drop down.
- The weight classes and the class quiz now sit in the Weigh-in menu. The menu reads Rules, Weight classes, Practice and Class quiz.
- The athlete cards, the lifting order and the Marshals' table now sit in one Chief Marshal menu. The top bar has two tools: Weigh-in and Chief Marshal. Old links still work.

## 0.5.0 - 2026-11-27

### Added
- Chief Marshal: an overview of what the Chief Marshal checks on every card and the rules behind it.
- Chief Marshal: a practice at the Marshals' table. Two athletes, or three or four if you choose, lift a snatch with a running clock, with the athlete cards side by side, and coaches hand in changes of weight. You accept or decline each one, and your decisions are checked when the athlete raises the bar.

## 0.4.0 - 2026-11-26

### Added
- Lifting order: an overview of the four factors that decide who is called next, with the rulebook's own example group.
- Lifting order: a practice where you call a group of 6 to 10 athletes in the right order through the snatch or the clean & jerk. A wrong call tells you which factor decides.

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-26

### Added
- Weigh-in: an overview of the weigh-in rules, with the 250 g deducted for the costume.
- Weigh-in: a practice where you read the scale, work out the bodyweight and decide whether the athlete makes weight, is too heavy or is too light.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-26

### Added
- Athlete cards: the Simulation page takes a random athlete through the snatch and the clean & jerk, attempt by attempt, with the 20 kg Rule and the total.
- In the Simulation the athlete can abstain from the rest of a lift or withdraw from the competition.
- An About page that says what the site is for, which rules it uses and who made it.
- A What's new page with the version number, and a New marker when the site has changed since your last visit.
- A Source on GitHub link in the bottom right corner.

### Changed
- The front page has an intro and lists every page of every tool.
- After a no lift the card strikes the weight through instead of circling it.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-25

### Added
- Weight classes: an overview of the senior men's and women's bodyweight classes.
- Weight classes: a training game where you pick the valid classes from a grid of 5 x 5 to 10 x 10 tiles, with Check answers and Reveal answers.
- Athlete cards: the initial card at weigh-in for a random athlete, with starting weights that respect the 20 kg Rule and a Reset button for a new athlete.
- Light and dark mode, following your system by default.
